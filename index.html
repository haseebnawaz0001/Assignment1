About Our Machines

Bole Machinery builds servo energy-saving injection moulding machines for factories that need high precision and low running costs. Our machines use German-engineered central clamping technology.

Why Choose Bole?

Energy Saving

The servo motor only uses power when it is needed, so electricity use can drop by up to 60%.

Central Clamping

German-engineered central clamping spreads the force evenly across the mould.

High Precision

Accurate control gives every part the same size and quality.
